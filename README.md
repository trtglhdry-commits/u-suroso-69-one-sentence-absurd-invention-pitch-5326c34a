One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday
annoyance — a fridge that judges your midnight snacking. That sentence is
the whole entry: no code, no explanation.

## The pitch

> A fridge that dims its own light and whispers 'again?' every time you open it after midnight, so your 2 a.m. snack comes with a witness.

136 characters, one sentence.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) holds the pitch as a constant, enforces its own
220-character gate (1–220 characters, not whitespace-only), and writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Deterministic:
every run writes byte-identical output.
